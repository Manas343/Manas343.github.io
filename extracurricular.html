Community Service & Healthcare

Association with Divyang Punarvas and Swasthya Kendra (Differently abled Rehabilitation and Health Centre) run by Bharat Vikas Parishad in Hisar since 2024.

  • Voluntary service of 30 hrs at Physiotherapy Lab for Children with Cerebral Palsy in Jul-Aug 2026.
  • Completed 60 hours of voluntary service at Divyang Punarvas and Swastha Kendra in May-June 2025, which included a weeklong training program in the prosthetics lab and artificial limbs workshop.
